*** Question 5

** Part (a)

	gen lpyrs=log(pyrs)
	
	** Model without age
		glm deaths, family(poisson) offset(lpyrs) link(log)
			est store A

	** Model with age (categorical)
		glm deaths i.agegrp, family(poisson) offset(lpyrs) link(log)
			est store B
			
	** Compare models
		lrtest A B

	** We can calculate this p-value ourselves by first getting the difference
	* in log likelihoods,a nd multiplying by -2:
		dis (-70.3626661 - -19.27674826) * -2
	
	* and then we refer to a chi-square distribution with 3 df (the age group
	* variable has four levels, so the second model is calculating an extra 
	* three parameters).
		dis chi2tail(3,102.172)

** Part (b)
* For illustration purposes, we're going to switch to the poisson command now.

	** i) 60-69 vs 50-59
		poisson deaths i.agegrp , e(pyrs)
			dis exp(1.582834) // Estimate
			dis exp(1.004549) // Lower end of CI
			dis exp(2.161118) // Upper end of CI
		* Confirm the poisson command gives the same results as glm
		glm deaths i.agegrp, family(poisson) offset(lpyrs) link(log)

		** In glm we use the "eform" option to get rates; in Poisson we use irr
		glm deaths i.agegrp, family(poisson) offset(lpyrs) link(log) eform
		poisson deaths i.agegrp , e(pyrs) irr

	** ii) 80-89 vs 70-79
		* Using lincom:
		poisson deaths i.agegrp, e(pyrs)
			lincom 4.agegrp - 3.agegrp , irr
				** Stata note: lincom continues to accept the eform and irr options
				lincom 4.agegrp - 3.agegrp , eform

		** Re-parameterise the model so that 70-79 is the baseline group
			poisson deaths b3.agegrp, e(pyrs) irr
